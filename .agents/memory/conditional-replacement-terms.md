---
name: Explicit replacement conditional terms
description: Owner decision for conditional discounts after corrections or prior credits.
---

Require explicit replacement terms. Preserve the original agreement; record newly
agreed discount and deadline with evidence and review the resulting threshold.
Do not automatically scale discounts, retrospectively qualify earlier payments,
or reset an already-consumed agreement. Unsupported histories reject safely.

**Why:** The owner selected this rule on 2026-11-06 rather than leaving the
combinations permanently blocked.

**How to apply:** Implement in the isolated candidate. This is not authority to
enable public posting, modify the live demo, deploy, or claim independent review.

Stage history and financial admission separately. While replacement history is
available before versioned redemption, reject redemption of superseded terms.

**Why:** Recording new terms must not leave the old agreement financially usable
in the gap between the owner's sequential implementation jobs.

**How to apply:** Keep the interim fence until the versioned payment, observation
and redemption lifecycle has its own acceptance evidence; do not remove it merely
because history capture works.

Keep the original posting-binding hash as the permanent consumption identity.
Replacement IDs and frozen hashes identify agreement versions, not new originals.

**Why:** A replacement must not create another redemption allowance after the
original has been consumed, even if the credit is subsequently compensated.

**How to apply:** Carry the version in frozen payment evidence, reject prior
cash for later terms, and retain the original immutable consumption boundary.

After a correction, show the current outstanding balance while preserving posted
credits. The owner confirmed: invoice £120 minus credit £24 gives £96 outstanding;
correcting the invoice face value to £100 gives £76 outstanding. The credit stays £24.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed updating the outstanding balance, not
rewriting posted credit history.

**How to apply:** Corrections retain credited amounts and per-line capacity;
customer-facing credit views should show the current linked outstanding balance.
