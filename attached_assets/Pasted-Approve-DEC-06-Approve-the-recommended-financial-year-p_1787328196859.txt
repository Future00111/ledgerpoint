DEC-06 — FINANCIAL-YEAR POLICY

STATUS: APPROVED

1. DEFAULT FINANCIAL YEAR

For new UK-focused companies, Ledgerpoint's default financial year is:

1 April → 31 March

This is a product default, not an immutable requirement.

2. COMPANY-CONFIGURABLE START

A company may select another valid recurring financial-year start during company setup or before its first canonical posted accounting record.

The architecture must support company-specific financial-year starts without hard-coding 1 April as the only possible value.

3. FINANCIAL-YEAR IDENTITY

Financial-year identity must be explicitly represented and company-scoped.

It must remain distinct from:

- document dates
- transaction dates
- VAT periods
- tax years
- bank dates.

Do not infer financial-year identity solely from a document date at reporting time.

4. POSTED-RECORD BOUNDARY

Once canonical posted accounting records exist, the ordinary company configuration must NOT allow the financial-year start to be changed retrospectively.

Do not silently reclassify existing posted accounting records into different financial years.

Any future financial-year transition/change mechanism must be separately designed, approved, privileged and fully audited.

5. FIRST FINANCIAL YEAR

Support:

- first financial year
- partial first financial year where applicable
- normal recurring subsequent years
- year-boundary transitions.

Do not assume the first financial year must always be a full 12 months.

6. HISTORICAL DATA

Historical financial years must be represented using available source evidence.

Ledgerpoint must NEVER invent historical accounting data merely to populate financial-year structures.

Where historical data is incomplete or ambiguous, record the limitation rather than manufacture accounting facts.

7. REPORTING

Financial-year identity must support accurate:

- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Trial Balance
- General Ledger
- comparative reporting

and other accounting reports.

Reporting must ultimately rely on the canonical accounting model approved under DEC-04.

8. PERMISSIONS AND AUDIT

Changing the company's financial-year configuration is a consequential accounting configuration action.

It must therefore respect DEC-05:

- active company membership
- company scope
- server-side capability checks
- appropriate privileged authority
- audit trail.

9. FUTURE FLEXIBILITY

Do not unnecessarily prevent future support for:

- different financial-year starts
- additional markets
- additional currencies
- future international accounting requirements.

DEC-06 establishes the initial UK product policy; it does not permanently restrict future product evolution.

10. DEC-07 / DEC-08 BOUNDARY

DEC-06 does NOT decide:

- accounting-period frequency
- period creation rules
- period closing
- period reopening
- detailed period controls
- year-end closing mechanics
- year-end journal treatment
- retained earnings mechanics.

Those remain for DEC-07 and DEC-08 respectively.
