PHASE 4B – AI MISSING REVENUE & MISSING INVOICE DETECTION

Build the next component of the Ledgerly AI Accountant.

The objective is to automatically identify money received into a bank account where Ledgerly cannot find a corresponding invoice, or where the payment amount does not reconcile correctly against available invoices.

IMPORTANT:

Do not replace the existing reconciliation system.

Extend the existing reconciliation and AI Accountant architecture.

Reuse existing database models, services, AI services and UI components wherever possible.

Do not create duplicate accounting logic.

--------------------------------------------------
1. PAYMENT-TO-INVOICE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------

Whenever a bank transaction is received:

Analyse the transaction against all relevant invoices.

Consider:

- Exact invoice reference
- Customer name
- Customer account
- Amount
- Payment date
- Invoice date
- Invoice status
- Partial payments
- Multiple invoices paid together
- Customer payment references
- Bank transaction description

Calculate a confidence score.

Example:

Bank transaction:

£12,500 received

Customer:

ABC Construction

Potential invoices:

INV-1045 £10,000
INV-1062 £2,500

Result:

100% of payment accounted for.

Recommendation:

"Payment appears to settle 2 invoices."

--------------------------------------------------
2. MULTIPLE INVOICE MATCHING
--------------------------------------------------

The system must be capable of matching one bank transaction against multiple invoices.

Example:

Payment received:

£15,000

Invoices:

INV-1001 £10,000
INV-1002 £5,000

Result:

£15,000 matched.

Status:

READY TO APPROVE

Do not require the user to manually split the payment.

4. UNMATCHED REVENUE DETECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Example:

Payment received:

£12,500

Available invoices:

£10,000

Result:

£10,000 matched.

£2,500 unmatched.

Create an AI Accountant task:

Type:

missing_invoice

Title:

"£2,500 of received revenue is not matched to an invoice"

Description:

"I found a £12,500 payment from ABC Construction. Available invoices account for £10,000 of this payment. £2,500 remains unexplained."

Show:

Payment received: £12,500

Matched invoices: £10,000

Unmatched revenue: £2,500

Confidence: 94%

Recommended action:

"Review payment and create or locate the missing invoice."

--------------------------------------------------
10. AI EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------

Every missing-revenue task must explain WHY it was created.

Example:

"£2,500 appears to be missing from your invoicing."

"£12,500 was received from ABC Construction."

"I found invoices totalling £10,000 that could be matched to this payment."

"£2,500 remains unexplained."

"Possible explanations:

• An invoice has not been created.
• The payment relates to another customer.
• The customer has overpaid.
• Several invoices may have been combined."

--------------------------------------------------
14. IMPORTANT ACCOUNTING SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------

The AI may recommend creating an invoice.

It must NOT automatically create or post an invoice without explicit user approval.

The AI may identify missing revenue.

It must NOT assume that every unmatched bank receipt represents revenue.

Possible explanations must remain available.

--------------------------------------------------
16. PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Do not send every invoice to OpenAI individually.

Perform deterministic matching first.

Only use AI for ambiguous cases and explanations.

Cache analysis where appropriate.

Minimise OpenAI API usage and token consumption.
