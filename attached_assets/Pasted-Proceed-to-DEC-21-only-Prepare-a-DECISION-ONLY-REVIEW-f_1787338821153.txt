==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Determine Ledgerly's tenant/company isolation policy.

The review must establish how Ledgerly guarantees that one company's:

- accounting;
- customers;
- suppliers;
- documents;
- payments;
- bank information;
- configuration;
- audit records;
- exports;
- backups/recovery;
- AI context;

cannot improperly become visible to or affect another company.

The policy must distinguish:

- company scope;
- user membership;
- capability/authority;
- authentication;
- authorisation;
- database/data isolation;
- service-layer enforcement;
- background jobs;
- AI context;
- exports;
- backups;
- support/admin access.

Do not turn this into an implementation task.

==================================================
1. COMPANY BOUNDARY
==================================================

Define the authoritative company/tenant boundary.

Determine:

- what constitutes a company;
- whether all accounting data belongs to exactly one company;
- whether users may belong to multiple companies;
- how active company membership affects access;
- how company context is established;
- whether records may ever legitimately span companies.

Respect DEC-05.

Company scope must be explicit.

==================================================
2. AUTHORISATION
==================================================

Respect DEC-05 server-side capabilities.

Determine how tenant isolation interacts with:

- user identity;
- active membership;
- role/capability;
- company scope;
- resource ownership.

A user having authority in Company A must not imply authority in Company B.

Do not rely on client-side company selection.

==================================================
3. DATA MODEL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Determine the policy expectation for company ownership of data.

Consider whether authoritative company-owned records should have explicit company identity and whether cross-company references are permitted.

Do not prescribe an implementation schema unless required to express the policy.

==================================================

- customer credits;
- supplier prepayments;
- refunds;

==================================================
11. AI
==================================================

Determine tenant-isolation requirements for AI.

AI must never use Company A information to answer a Company B request unless there is an explicitly approved legitimate cross-company context.

Consider:

- prompts;
- uploaded documents;
- accounting context;
- retrieved records;
- memory/context;
- AI-generated suggestions;
- AI action history.

AI must remain company-scoped.

Do not assume external AI providers or architecture unless documented elsewhere.
