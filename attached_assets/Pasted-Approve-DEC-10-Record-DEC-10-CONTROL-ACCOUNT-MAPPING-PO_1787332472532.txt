DEC-10 — CONTROL-ACCOUNT MAPPING POLICY
STATUS: APPROVED

Approve the recommended protected, company-scoped control-account mapping model.

1. ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE

Each company will have one authoritative Accounts Receivable control account for normal customer receivable accounting.

The mapping is protected and company-scoped.

It must be used consistently by canonical customer receivable postings.

2. ACCOUNTS PAYABLE

Each company will have one authoritative Accounts Payable control account for normal supplier payable accounting.

The mapping is protected and company-scoped.

It must be used consistently by canonical supplier payable postings.

3. BANK / CASH

Each accounting bank/cash location may have its own protected accounting account mapping.

Bank-feed evidence must remain distinct from the canonical accounting bank/cash account and from the accounting journal itself, in accordance with DEC-04.

4. VAT

The approved VAT control mappings are:

- one Output VAT account;
- one Input VAT account.

These mappings are protected and company-scoped.

VAT settlement may use a protected settlement role only where an approved VAT-return settlement workflow requires it.

Do not create unnecessary VAT control accounts beyond what the approved DEC-03 workflow requires.

DEC-03 remains the sole VAT authority.

5. OTHER ACCOUNTS

The following are NOT universal control accounts:

- Revenue
- Cost of Sales
- ordinary Expenses
- ordinary Assets
- general Equity.

These remain validated configurable account selections.

Do not unnecessarily restrict normal company chart customisation.

6. RETAINED EARNINGS

Retained earnings must NOT become a launch default control account.

This follows DEC-08's approved reporting-only year-end policy.

Do not create automatic retained-earnings postings.

Any future explicit retained-earnings accounting treatment requires a separately approved policy.

7. MAPPING CHANGES

Control-account mapping changes must be:

- prospective only;
- company-scoped;
- server-side capability-gated under DEC-05;
- validated;
- reasoned where required;
- auditable.

A mapping change must NEVER reinterpret historical accounting.

8. HISTORICAL JOURNALS

Historical posted journals retain their resolved account identity.

Changing a current control-account mapping must NOT:

- rewrite historical journal lines;
- move historical balances;
- change historical VAT results;
- change historical AR/AP balances;
- reinterpret historical postings.

9. CONFIGURATION VERSIONING BOUNDARY

DEC-11 remains responsible for the complete configuration-versioning and effective-dating policy.

DEC-10 establishes the accounting principle that mapping changes are prospective and historical accounting remains unchanged.

Do not resolve the complete DEC-11 mechanism here.

10. DEC-05 INTEGRATION

All consequential mapping changes must use the approved DEC-05 architecture:

- active company membership;
- company scope;
- server-side capability;
- appropriate authority;
- audit evidence.

Do not rely solely on role names or frontend controls.

11. REPORTING

Reports must derive from the canonical DEC-04 journal model and the account identity resolved at posting time.

Do not recalculate historical accounting using the company's current mapping configuration.

12. MIGRATION

Historical mappings must be migrated using available evidence.

Never invent:

- AR mappings;
- AP mappings;
- bank mappings;
- VAT mappings;
- historical account relationships.

Ambiguous mappings must become explicit migration exceptions for DEC-22.

13. FUTURE FLEXIBILITY

The model must allow future changes to:

- company mappings;
- chart structures;
- VAT workflows;
- payment workflows;
- additional markets;
- additional currencies.

Future changes must follow the Living Product Decisions process.
