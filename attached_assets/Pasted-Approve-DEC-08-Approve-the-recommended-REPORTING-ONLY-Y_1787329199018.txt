REPORTING-ONLY YEAR-END POLICY

Record DEC-08 as APPROVED with the following policy:

1. YEAR-END METHOD

Ledgerpoint will use a reporting-only year-end model at launch.

Do NOT automatically create:
- year-end closing journals;
- retained-earnings transfer journals;
- artificial year-end accounting postings.

Financial-year boundaries affect reporting and financial-year presentation without mutating canonical journal history.

2. TEMPORARY AND PERMANENT ACCOUNTS

Revenue, cost of sales and expense accounts are treated as temporary for financial-year reporting purposes.

Assets, liabilities and equity continue across financial years.

Do not physically zero or rewrite posted journal balances at year-end.

3. PROFIT / LOSS

Current-year profit/loss and prior-year results are derived from the canonical accounting records through authoritative reporting.

Any accumulated-results or retained-earnings presentation must remain traceable to the underlying accounting data.

Do not manufacture accounting journals solely to produce a reporting balance.

4. RETAINED EARNINGS

Do not create an automatic retained-earnings posting at year-end.

If a future product/accounting policy requires explicit retained-earnings journals, that must be introduced through a separately approved decision and implemented as normal canonical accounting postings.

5. YEAR-END COMPLETION

All DEC-07 accounting periods belonging to the financial year must be CLOSED before the year-end review can be completed.

Year-end completion is a derived condition plus an auditable year-end review/completion event.

Do NOT create a separate overlapping accounting-period state solely for year-end completion.

6. WARNINGS VS BLOCKERS

The following remain visible warnings unless another approved policy later determines otherwise:

- drafts;
- unallocated payments;
- unreconciled banking;
- ordinary VAT timetable differences.

Genuine accounting or VAT integrity failures may block year-end completion.

Do not invent additional mandatory blockers.

7. YEAR-END AUTHORITY

Year-end review/completion must use:

- active company membership;
- company scope;
- server-side capability enforcement;
- appropriate DEC-05 authority;
- audit evidence.

Do not rely on UI-only controls.

8. POST-YEAR-END CORRECTIONS

Errors discovered after year-end must be handled through explicit accounting mechanisms.

Permitted mechanisms may include:

- controlled period reopening;
- explicit correction/reversal;
- approved later-period adjustment where appropriate.

Never:
- rewrite posted journals;
- delete historical accounting;
- silently change posting dates;
- silently reassign historical financial years;
- overwrite historical balances.

9. VAT

DEC-03 remains the authoritative VAT policy and VAT engine.

Year-end must not:
- create a second VAT engine;
- alter VAT calculations;
- silently change VAT-period treatment;
- expand the approved VAT scope.

10. HISTORICAL MIGRATION

Historical migration must never invent:

- closing journals;
- retained-earnings postings;
- year-end approvals;
- year-end events;
- accounting dates;
- accounting facts.

Where historical evidence is incomplete, preserve the limitation and handle it through the later migration decision.

11. REPORTING

The year-end architecture must support authoritative:

- Profit & Loss;
- Balance Sheet;
- Trial Balance;
- General Ledger;
- comparative reporting;
- prior-year reporting;
- retained-results presentation.

Reports remain subordinate to the DEC-04 canonical accounting journal.

12. FUTURE FLEXIBILITY

This approval does NOT permanently prohibit explicit year-end accounting journals.

A future requirement for:
- explicit closing journals;
- explicit retained-earnings postings;
- alternative year-end mechanics

must be separately proposed, reviewed and approved through the Living Product Decisions process.

13. DECISION BOUNDARY

DEC-08 does NOT resolve:

- DEC-09 chart/default policy;
- DEC-10 control-account mappings;
- DEC-11 configuration versioning;
- DEC-12 through DEC-16 payment policies;
- DEC-17 through DEC-21 retention/export/backup/RLS policies;
- DEC-22 migration and cutover policy.
