==================================================
DEFECT 1 — CONFLICTING COMPANY CONTEXT
==================================================

Fix the two identified protected paths:

- POST /api/ai/reconciliation/analyse
- suggestTransactionMatches in the functions route

Where a request supplies both:

- a caller-supplied company_id
AND
- a resource identifier whose authoritative company can be resolved

the implementation MUST reject the request if the two company contexts differ.

Required behaviour:

Company A resource + Company A company_id
→ allowed, subject to normal authorization.

Company A resource + Company B company_id
→ FAIL CLOSED.

Company A resource + malformed/invalid company context
→ FAIL CLOSED.

Company A resource + missing company_id where resource ownership can safely establish company context
→ follow the approved server-derived resource-scope behaviour.

Do not silently replace a contradictory caller-supplied company_id with the resource's company.

The resource's authoritative company may establish scope only when the supplied company context is absent or consistent.

==================================================
DEFECT 2 — DUPLICATE ACTIVE MEMBERSHIPS
==================================================

Address the identified nondeterministic authorization risk involving:

company_users

and:

findActiveMembership(... limit(1))

The implementation must ensure that authorization cannot depend on an arbitrary active membership row.

Preferred approach:

- determine whether duplicate active membership records are possible under the existing model;
- ensure conflicting active membership states cannot result in nondeterministic authorization;
- fail closed if multiple active memberships for the same company/user produce conflicting authorization state.

Do NOT automatically introduce a schema constraint or migration.

If a database uniqueness constraint is genuinely required:

STOP before changing the schema and report:

- why the application-level solution is insufficient;
- the exact constraint required;
- existing-data implications;
- migration implications;
- whether duplicate records currently exist.

A schema/migration change requires separate approval.
