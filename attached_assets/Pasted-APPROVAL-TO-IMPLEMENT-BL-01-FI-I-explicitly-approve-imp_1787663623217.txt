APPROVAL TO IMPLEMENT — BL-01-FI

I explicitly approve implementation of:

BL-01-FI — Complete server-side active-membership and company-scope enforcement

This is the FIRST authorised implementation task.

==================================================
APPROVED SCOPE
==================================================

Implement only the scope defined in:

docs/ledgerly-first-implementation-approval-package.md

and the associated planning/design records for the Principal and Tenant Guard.

The objective is to establish server-side enforcement of:

- authenticated company context;
- active company membership;
- server-derived company scope;
- company/resource ownership;
- fail-closed behaviour when membership or company scope cannot be established;
- correct propagation of company context into applicable background jobs;
- protection against cross-company access.

==================================================
SCHEMA / DATA
==================================================

Use the existing membership and company information where possible.

==================================================
ACCOUNTING SAFETY
==================================================

This task must create NO accounting effects.

It must not create or modify:

- journals;
- journal lines;
- payments;
- allocations;
- VAT;
- AR/AP balances;
- refunds;
- financial periods;
- financial years;
- accounting configuration;
- control-account mappings.

==================================================
SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Enforcement must be server-side.

Do not rely on:

- frontend checks;
- hidden UI elements;
- client-provided company IDs;
- arbitrary role strings;
- client-side capability decisions.

Company context must be derived and validated server-side.

A user must not gain authority in Company A merely because they have authority in Company B.

Inactive/revoked membership must fail closed.

Cross-company resource access must fail closed.

Where background jobs are involved, preserve and validate the originating company context.

==================================================
TESTING
==================================================

Implement the acceptance tests defined in the approved task package, including at minimum:

- active member can access their authorised company context;
- inactive/revoked member is denied;
- user cannot access another company's resources;
- company scope cannot be overridden by client input;
- missing/invalid company context fails closed;
- background jobs preserve company context;
- cross-company job access fails;
- authorised access does not create accounting side effects;
- existing behaviour outside the task scope remains intact.

Add appropriate regression tests.
