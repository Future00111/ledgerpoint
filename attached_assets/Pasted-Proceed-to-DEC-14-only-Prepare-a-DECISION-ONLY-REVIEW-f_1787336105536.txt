==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Determine Ledgerly's detailed USER WORKFLOW for handling unapplied cash.

DEC-13 has already established the accounting representation:

- customer excess/customer credit is a liability;
- supplier excess/supplier prepayment is an asset;
- known-party payments may exist before document allocation;
- bank evidence remains separate;
- allocation must not exceed eligible amounts.

DEC-14 must now determine how users identify, review, manage and allocate these balances.

Do NOT use DEC-14 to resolve:

- refund policy;
- refund source;
- refund VAT;
- refund approval;
- payment-on-account launch scope.

Those remain for DEC-15 and DEC-16.

==================================================
1. DEFINITION OF UNAPPLIED CASH
==================================================

Define precisely what Ledgerly means by:

- unapplied customer payment;
- unapplied supplier payment;
- unallocated payment remainder;
- customer credit;
- supplier prepayment;
- bank evidence awaiting reconciliation.

Ensure these are not conflated.

==================================================
2. WORKFLOW ENTRY POINTS
==================================================

Determine how users encounter unapplied cash.

Consider:

- payment creation;
- bank reconciliation;
- customer account;
- supplier account;
- invoice/bill screens;
- dedicated unapplied-cash workspace;
- notifications;
- Ask.

Recommend the most appropriate launch experience.

==================================================
3. KNOWN PARTY VS UNKNOWN PARTY
==================================================

Define the workflow for:

KNOWN PARTY + KNOWN PAYMENT

KNOWN PARTY + PAYMENT BUT NO DOCUMENT

UNKNOWN PARTY + BANK EVIDENCE

UNKNOWN PARTY + ACCOUNTING PAYMENT

Determine when an accounting payment can be created and when evidence must remain unresolved.

Do not silently turn unknown bank evidence into accounting.

==================================================
4. CUSTOMER WORKFLOW
==================================================

Define the workflow when a customer payment cannot yet be allocated.

Consider:

- payment received;
- customer identified;
- no invoice identified;
- partial allocation;
- remaining credit;
- later invoice creation;
- later allocation.

Explain what the user sees and what actions are available.

==================================================
5. SUPPLIER WORKFLOW
==================================================

Define the equivalent workflow for supplier payments.

Consider:

- supplier identified;
- no bill identified;
- partial allocation;
- remaining prepayment;
- later bill creation;
- later allocation.

==================================================
6. ALLOCATION WORKFLOW
==================================================

Determine how a user allocates unapplied money.

Consider:

- selecting one document;
- selecting multiple documents;
- partial allocation;
- allocation amount;
- remaining balance;
- validation;
- confirmation;
- audit.

Allocation must respect DEC-12 limits.

==================================================
7. REALLOCATION / UNALLOCATION
==================================================

Determine how users:

- remove an allocation;
- move an allocation;
- partially reallocate;
- return an amount to unapplied status.

Preserve append-only history.

Do not allow silent accounting mutation.

==================================================
8. SEARCH AND MATCHING
==================================================

Determine whether Ledgerly should suggest matching documents.

Consider matching signals such as:

- customer/supplier;
- amount;
- reference;
- invoice/bill number;
- date;
- bank reference.

If AI or automated matching is considered:

- rules must remain authoritative;
- AI may recommend;
- users remain in control;
- consequential accounting actions require appropriate capability.

Respect the Ledgerly Manifesto.

==================================================
9. AUTO-ALLOCATION
==================================================

Evaluate:

- manual allocation;
- suggested allocation;
- automatic allocation.

If automatic allocation is recommended, define:

- confidence threshold;
- deterministic rules;
- user visibility;
- audit;
- reversal;
- safeguards.

Do not silently automate financial decisions.

==================================================
10. PARTIAL PAYMENTS
==================================================

Define how the workflow handles:

- partial invoice payment;
- partial bill payment;
- multiple payments;
- payment remainder;
- document remainder.

Settlement remains derived under DEC-12.

==================================================
11. CUSTOMER CREDIT / SUPPLIER PREPAYMENT

Define how the user sees:

- customer credit balance;
- supplier prepayment balance.

Do not present them as ordinary aged receivables/payables.

Ensure users understand:

- what the balance represents;
- why it exists;
- what they can do next.

==================================================
12. REPORTING AND STATEMENTS
==================================================

Determine how unapplied/customer-credit/supplier-prepayment amounts appear in:

- customer statements;
- supplier statements;
- customer balance;
- supplier balance;
- aged receivables;
- aged payables;
- Balance Sheet;
- Trial Balance;
- General Ledger.

Do not create contradictory balances between screens.

==================================================
13. PERIOD CONTROLS
==================================================

Respect DEC-07.

Determine how:

- payment posting;
- allocation;
- unallocation;
- reallocation

interact with open and closed periods.

Do not allow workflow actions to bypass accounting-period controls.

==================================================
14. ACCOUNTING EFFECTS
==================================================

Respect DEC-13.

Determine when the workflow creates:

- the original payment journal;
- customer-credit liability reclassification;
- supplier-prepayment asset reclassification.

Do not create duplicate cash/AR/AP postings.
