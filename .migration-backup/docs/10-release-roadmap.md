# 10 — Release Roadmap

Track future releases. Every new feature is assigned to a release here.

---

## Version 1.0 — MVP

Core UK accounting for small businesses and garages.

- Invoicing (sales invoices)
- Bills (purchase bills)
- Credit notes (sales & supplier)
- Bank accounts & transactions (manual + CSV)
- VAT returns (9-box with drill-down)
- Documents & AI extraction (review before saving)
- Email capture (mock scan)
- Chart of accounts (business-type defaults)
- General ledger & manual journals
- Reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance)
- Customers & suppliers
- Multi-company
- Users & permissions
- Setup wizard
- AI Accounting Copilot (read-only)
- AI Insights dashboard
- Smart Account Suggestions
- Ask (universal assistant)
- Dashboard (Business Command Centre, role modes)
- Customer Safe Mode
- Development Tools (gated)

## Version 1.1

- Open Banking live feed
- Recurring invoices & bills
- Document inbox sharing
- HMRC MTD prep / pre-submission validation
- More dashboard widgets
- Custom report templates

## Version 2.0

- HMRC MTD direct VAT submission
- Online invoice payments
- Project / job costing
- Custom reports builder
- Mobile companion app

## Future

- Multi-currency
- Payroll
- Inventory / stock
- Budget vs actual
- Consolidated / inter-company reporting
- AI predictive forecasts

---

## Assignment rules

- Every new PRD feature must be assigned to a release before implementation.
- Features move **Future → v2.0 → v1.1 → v1.0** as they are prioritised.
- Status of each idea is tracked in [04 Future Ideas](./04-future-ideas.md).
