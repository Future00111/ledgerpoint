# 04 — Future Ideas

Store ideas that are not part of Version 1. Ideas should never be lost.

Each idea includes: **Description**, **Business Value**, **Complexity**, **Priority**, and **Status**.

**Status values:** Idea · Researching · Planned · In Progress · Deferred · Rejected

**Priority values:** Low · Medium · High

---

## Open Banking Live Feed

- **Description:** Connect bank accounts via Open Banking (TrueLayer / Yapily) for automatic transaction import.
- **Business Value:** Eliminates manual CSV import; always-current balances.
- **Complexity:** High
- **Priority:** High
- **Status:** Planned (v1.1)
