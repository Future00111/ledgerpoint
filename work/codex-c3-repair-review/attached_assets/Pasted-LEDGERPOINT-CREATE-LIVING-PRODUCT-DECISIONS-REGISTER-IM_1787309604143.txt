LEDGERPOINT — CREATE LIVING PRODUCT DECISIONS REGISTER

IMPORTANT:
This is a PRODUCT GOVERNANCE task only.

DO NOT implement application features.
DO NOT modify the database.
DO NOT modify APIs.
DO NOT modify accounting logic.
DO NOT modify UI.
DO NOT install dependencies.
DO NOT publish or deploy.

We are deliberately creating a living product-decision system that allows Ledgerpoint to evolve without allowing the coding agent to make product decisions autonomously.

==================================================
1. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The Ledgerly Manifesto remains the highest-level authority.

The Manifesto is intentionally stable and defines:
- why the product exists
- product beliefs
- accounting-first principles
- AI boundaries
- design philosophy
- UX principles
- engineering principles.

The product itself must remain capable of evolving.

Therefore:

DO NOT treat every product decision as permanently locked.

A product decision can be deliberately revisited, changed or deferred.

However, the coding agent must NEVER silently change an approved product decision.

Any change to product direction must be explicitly recorded.

==================================================
2. DOCUMENT HIERARCHY
==================================================

Maintain the following authority hierarchy:

1. Ledgerly Manifesto
2. Product Principles
3. PRD / Product Scope
4. Technical Architecture
5. Product Gap Analysis / Feature Matrix
6. Master Backlog
7. Living Product Decisions Register
8. Approved implementation task
9. Existing implementation/code

If documents conflict:

- Higher-level principles take precedence.
- Product decisions may evolve through the decision process.
- Existing code must NOT be treated as the authority over approved product requirements.

==================================================
3. CREATE A LIVING PRODUCT DECISIONS REGISTER
==================================================

Locate any existing product decision/governance document first.

If an appropriate document already exists, extend it rather than creating a duplicate.

If none exists, create an appropriate document such as:

docs/ledgerly-product-decisions.md

Do not create multiple competing decision registers.

==================================================
4. DECISION STATUSES
==================================================

Every decision must have one of these statuses:

APPROVED
Current intended product decision.

PROVISIONAL
Current direction, but deliberately allowed to change as the product develops.

DEFERRED
A decision is required eventually, but there is no reason to decide it yet.

OPEN
No decision has been made.

FUTURE
Explicitly outside the current product scope but potentially relevant later.

REVISIT
A previous decision should be deliberately reassessed at a specified stage.

Do not use "LOCKED" as a permanent state.

The product is allowed to evolve.

==================================================
5. DECISION PRINCIPLES
==================================================

For every decision, record:

- Decision ID
- Subject
- Current status
- Current decision/direction
- Reason
- Impact
- Dependencies
- What it affects
- When it must be revisited
- Who/what can change it
- Date recorded
- Related backlog items
- Related architecture decisions where applicable

Keep the explanation concise and useful.

PD-20 — AI BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------

Status:
APPROVED

AI is an assistant.

AI may:
- analyse
- explain

- classify
- identify anomalies
- prepare drafts
- assist with repetitive work.

AI must NOT silently:
- make irreversible financial decisions
- post consequential accounting transactions without the required control
- submit VAT returns without the required approval/control

- modify locked records
- send consequential external financial communications without the required approval/control.

Rules before AI.

AI before asking the user.

Explain every suggestion.

Never automate silently.

These principles must not be weakened by implementation convenience.

--------------------------------------------------
7. PRODUCT EVOLUTION RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Ledgerpoint is a living product.

A decision may be changed when:

- new user evidence becomes available
- technical constraints change
- regulatory requirements change
- business requirements change
- product strategy changes
- a better solution is discovered.

When changing a decision:

1. Identify the existing decision.
2. Explain why it is being reconsidered.
3. Assess affected architecture/features/backlog items.
4. Record the new decision.
5. Update affected documentation.
6. Identify required migration/rework.
7. Only then implement the change.

Never silently change product direction through code.
