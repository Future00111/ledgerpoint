DEC-14 — UNAPPLIED CASH WORKFLOW POLICY
STATUS: APPROVED

Approve the recommended dedicated Unapplied Cash workflow with the following boundaries:

1. DEDICATED WORKSPACE

Ledgerly will provide a dedicated Unapplied Cash workspace.

The workspace should provide a clear place for users to identify, review and resolve:

- unapplied customer payments;
- unapplied supplier payments;
- customer credits;
- supplier prepayments;
- remaining payment balances.

2. CONTEXTUAL ENTRY POINTS

The workflow should also be accessible contextually from appropriate areas including:

- bank reconciliation;
- customer accounts;
- supplier accounts;
- invoices;
- bills;
- payment details;
- relevant notifications;
- Ask.

The dedicated workspace remains the authoritative user workflow for managing unapplied cash.

3. DISTINCT CONCEPTS

The user experience must clearly distinguish:

- bank evidence;
- accounting payment;
- unapplied balance;
- customer credit;
- supplier prepayment;
- allocation;
- refund;
- settlement.

Do not present these as interchangeable statuses.

4. KNOWN-PARTY PAYMENTS

A known-party accounting payment may exist before a specific invoice or bill is identified.

Ledgerly must not create a fake invoice or bill simply to provide an allocation target.

5. UNKNOWN BANK EVIDENCE

Unknown bank evidence remains unresolved until an authorised accounting classification exists.

Do not silently convert unknown bank evidence into an accounting payment.

6. CUSTOMER WORKFLOW

Provide a dedicated customer workflow for:

- identifying customer payments;
- reviewing unapplied amounts;
- allocating to eligible invoices;
- partially allocating;
- leaving a remaining customer credit;
- later allocating that credit.

Customer credits must remain clearly separate from ordinary aged receivables.

7. SUPPLIER WORKFLOW

Provide the equivalent supplier workflow for:

- identifying supplier payments;
- reviewing unapplied amounts;
- allocating to eligible bills;
- partially allocating;
- retaining a supplier prepayment;
- later allocating that prepayment.

Supplier prepayments must remain clearly separate from ordinary aged payables.

8. ELIGIBILITY RULES

Allocation eligibility must be determined by deterministic accounting/business rules.

At minimum respect DEC-12:

- allocation cannot exceed the payment remainder;
- allocation cannot exceed the eligible document open amount.

Rules are authoritative.

9. MATCHING SUGGESTIONS

Ledgerly may provide explainable matching suggestions using appropriate evidence such as:

- party;
- amount;
- reference;
- document number;
- date;
- bank reference;
- other approved matching signals.

Suggestions must be understandable to the user.

10. AI ROLE

AI may:

- recommend matches;
- explain why a match is suggested;
- help users find relevant invoices/bills;
- explain unapplied balances;
- help users understand what action is available.

AI must NOT:

- override deterministic accounting rules;
- create an invalid allocation;
- allocate silently;
- make consequential financial decisions without the required user action and capability.

This follows the Ledgerly Manifesto:

AI assists.
Rules determine accounting validity.
Users remain in control.

11. NO SILENT AUTO-ALLOCATION

Do NOT introduce silent automatic payment allocation at launch.

A suggested allocation must require appropriate user confirmation before the consequential accounting action occurs.

Future automated allocation may be considered through a separate approved policy.

12. MANUAL ALLOCATION

Users with the appropriate DEC-05 capability may manually allocate eligible unapplied amounts.

Support:

- full allocation;
- partial allocation;
- one payment → multiple documents;
- multiple payments → one document;
- remaining unapplied balances.

13. UNALLOCATION

Users may remove an allocation subject to the appropriate capability and accounting-period controls.

Unallocation must preserve append-only history.

Do not silently delete historical allocation events.

14. REALLOCATION

Users may reallocate eligible amounts between documents subject to:

- deterministic eligibility;
- available amount;
- document open amount;
- DEC-05 capability;
- DEC-07 period controls.

Preserve append-only reallocation history.

Do not repost the original cash/AR/AP payment merely because its allocation changes.

15. ACCOUNTING EFFECTS

Respect DEC-13.

Where a customer credit is later applied to AR, use the approved canonical reclassification:

DR Customer Credit / Receipt-on-Account Liability
CR Accounts Receivable

Where a supplier prepayment is later applied to AP, use:

DR Accounts Payable
CR Supplier Prepayment / Receivable Asset

Do not create duplicate cash postings.

16. SETTLEMENT

DEC-12 remains authoritative.

Settlement status must continue to be derived from authoritative payment, allocation, open-item and accounting information.

The workflow must not introduce an independently editable settlement state.

17. REPORTING

Customer credits must remain separate from aged receivables.

Supplier prepayments must remain separate from aged payables.

Ensure consistent presentation across:

- customer accounts;
- supplier accounts;
- statements;
- aged receivables;
- aged payables;
- Balance Sheet;
- Trial Balance;
- General Ledger.

18. PERIOD CONTROLS

All consequential allocation/reallocation/unallocation actions remain subject to DEC-07.

Do not permit workflow actions to bypass closed-period controls.

19. CONFIGURATION

Where an accounting reclassification depends on material accounting configuration:

- use the applicable DEC-11 configuration version;
- use approved DEC-10 mappings;
- retain resolved account IDs on canonical postings.

Do not redesign DEC-11.

20. PERMISSIONS

Use DEC-05 server-side capabilities for:

- allocation;
- unallocation;
- reallocation;
- clearing;
- other consequential unapplied-cash actions.

Do not rely on frontend visibility or arbitrary role strings.

21. AUDIT

Maintain immutable audit history for:

- payment identification;
- allocation;
- unallocation;
- reallocation;
- customer-credit creation;
- supplier-prepayment creation;
- clearing/adjustment.

Audit should preserve the relevant actor, company scope, action, reason where required, timestamp and accounting relationship.

22. REFUNDS

DEC-15 remains responsible for refund policy.

DEC-14 does NOT approve:

- whether refunds launch;
- refund source;
- refund approval;
- refund VAT treatment;
- refund reversal mechanics.

DEC-14 only establishes the workflow hand-off required for a future approved refund process.

23. PAYMENT-ON-ACCOUNT

DEC-16 remains responsible for user-facing payment-on-account scope.

DEC-14 does NOT approve or reject payment-on-account as a launch feature.

24. VAT

DEC-03 remains the sole VAT authority.

The unapplied-cash workflow must not create new VAT rules or a second VAT engine.

25. MIGRATION

Historical unapplied cash, customer credits and supplier prepayments may only be migrated where supported by evidence.

Never invent:

- historical allocations;
- historical customer credits;
- historical supplier prepayments;
- historical workflow actions;
- historical dates.

Ambiguity becomes an explicit DEC-22 migration exception.

26. FUTURE FLEXIBILITY

The workflow should not unnecessarily prevent future:

- automated matching;
- payment-on-account;
- refunds;
- additional payment methods;
- currencies;
- markets;
- AI-assisted workflows.

Future automation must follow the Ledgerly Manifesto and Living Product Decisions process.
