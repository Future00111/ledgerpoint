PHASE 4C – AI CUSTOMER COLLECTIONS & PAYMENT CHASING

Build the next component of the Ledgerly AI Accountant.

OBJECTIVE:

Ledgerly should automatically identify overdue customer invoices, assess the likelihood of payment, prioritise which customers need attention, and prepare appropriate payment-chasing actions.

The AI must act like an accounts receivable accountant.

IMPORTANT:

Do not replace the existing invoicing, customer, banking or AI systems.

Extend the existing architecture.

Reuse existing database models, services, components and AI infrastructure.

Do not duplicate existing functionality.

Do not automatically send customer communications without explicit user approval.

--------------------------------------------------
1. OVERDUE INVOICE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------

Analyse all outstanding customer invoices.

For each invoice determine:

- Invoice number
- Customer
- Invoice date
- Due date
- Invoice amount
- Amount paid
- Remaining balance
- Days overdue
- Previous payment behaviour
- Customer payment history
- Number of previous overdue invoices
- Average payment delay
- Current customer balance

--------------------------------------------------
2. CREATE AI COLLECTION TASKS
--------------------------------------------------

Create AI Accountant tasks for invoices requiring attention.

Task type:

customer_follow_up

Examples:

"Invoice INV-1042 is 18 days overdue."

"£4,850 remains outstanding."

"Customer normally pays within 7 days but this invoice is now 18 days overdue."

Priority:

critical
high
medium
low

--------------------------------------------------
3. CUSTOMER PAYMENT RISK
--------------------------------------------------

Calculate a payment-risk score.

Consider:

- Days overdue
- Amount outstanding
- Historical payment behaviour
- Previous late payments
- Number of overdue invoices
- Customer payment frequency
- Recent payment activity

Display:

LOW RISK

MEDIUM RISK

HIGH RISK

Do not imply that the customer will definitely fail to pay.

This is an AI assessment, not a credit decision.

9. AI PAYMENT REMINDER GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------

Add:

[Draft Reminder]

The AI should generate a professional payment reminder using the actual invoice information.

Example:

Subject:

Payment reminder – Invoice INV-1042

Message:

"Hi ABC Construction,

I hope you're well.

Just a quick reminder that invoice INV-1042 for £4,850 was due on 2 August and currently remains outstanding.

Could you please let us know when we can expect payment?

If payment has already been made, please disregard this message.

Kind regards,
[Company Name]"

Do not invent:

- Payment dates
- Customer contacts
- Bank details
- Invoice information

Use real Ledgerly data.

15. AI COLLECTION INSIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------

Generate useful insights such as:

--------------------------------------------------

"ABC Construction has been prioritised because:

• £12,500 remains outstanding.

• The customer has previously paid late.

--------------------------------------------------
18. SAFETY

The AI may:

✓ Identify overdue invoices
✓ Prioritise collections
✓ Analyse payment behaviour
✓ Draft reminders
✓ Recommend actions
✓ Generate collection insights

The AI must NOT:

✗ Automatically send emails
✗ Automatically call customers
✗ Change invoice amounts
✗ Change invoice due dates
✗ Write off debt
✗ Create credit notes automatically
✗ Alter accounting records without approval

--------------------------------------------------
19. PERFORMANCE

Use deterministic database queries for:

- Outstanding balances
- Days overdue
- Payment history
- Customer balances
- Invoice status

Use OpenAI only for:

- Explanations
- Risk interpretation
- Recommendations
- Message generation
- Natural-language responses

Do not send large accounting datasets unnecessarily to OpenAI.

Minimise token usage and API costs.
