# Prepare a bill or supplier credit from a source file

Draft help for the development version. Public release and real customer
onboarding have not been approved.

1. Select the company that owns the document, then open the
   [Document inbox](/documents).
2. Choose a PDF, JPEG or PNG source file. The inbox accepts up to 10 files at a
   time, each up to 5 MiB, and uploads them one at a time. Wait for the upload
   receipt before continuing. If an identical source already exists, open its
   existing record rather than creating another document.
3. Choose **Prepare bill** or **Prepare supplier credit** on the uploaded source.
   Enter and check the details against the original file. Automatic extraction
   and email intake are not connected; an upload does not transcribe the document.
4. Review the server-calculated totals and any warnings. Changing the document
   invalidates the previous review, so review the revised details again before
   saving the draft. A supplier credit must reference its original bill and retain
   the relevant original VAT evidence.
5. Save the reviewed draft. This preserves the source link. Saving or uploading
   does not post, pay, file or send the document. Use the document's separate
   protected review/posting workflow when it is ready.

If preparation actions are missing, check that the intended company is selected
and that your membership permits changes. An already linked source opens its
existing document. Do not bypass a permission or stale-review warning by creating
a replacement financial record.

Email account records and saved email rules do not connect a mailbox or run scans.
Use the Document inbox for manual source upload while those services are unavailable.

A public support contact and response commitment have not yet been configured.
Do not send financial documents or credentials to an unverified address.
